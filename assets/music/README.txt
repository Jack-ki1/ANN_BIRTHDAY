Place your .mp3 music files in this folder.

Required filenames (must match exactly):
  good-as-hell.mp3
  girls-just-wanna.mp3
  diamonds.mp3
  shake-it-off.mp3
  run-the-world.mp3
  thank-u-next.mp3
  love-on-top.mp3

To use different songs, edit the TRACKS array in js/player.js
and add the corresponding .mp3 files here.

Tip: You can download MP3s legally from:
  - https://www.jamendo.com  (free licensed music)
  - https://freemusicarchive.org
  - https://pixabay.com/music/
  - Purchase from iTunes / Amazon Music and use locally
